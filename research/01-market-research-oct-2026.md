# Roblox Market & Competitive Research — October 2026

*Compiled 2026-10-26. Purpose: find what the most viral and highest-earning Roblox games of the last week / month / 3 months have in common, so we can brainstorm our own game.*

> **Data caveat.** Roblox does not publish per-game revenue, and player counts come from third-party trackers (RBLXSTAT, Rolimons, RoWatcher, BloxRant), which disagree with each other by 10–50%. Steal an Egg's peak, for example, is listed as 12.4M, 14.2M and 14.27M depending on the source. Treat every number here as directional. Roblox reports Q3 results on **Oct 29, 2026**, so some platform figures will be out of date soon.

---

## 1. TL;DR

1. **The biggest hit of the window is *Steal An Egg and Collect Rare Pets*** (launched Jul 25). It is a remix of 2025's *Steal a Brainrot* stealing loop, with pets/eggs and a treadmill speed mechanic added. It reached a 12–14M peak CCU, had about 6B visits in Q3 alone, and is #2 on the Top Earning chart.
2. **The fast followers did almost as well as originals.** *Break and Steal an Egg* (Sep 5) peaked at 1.3M CCU, higher than every other 2026 launch except the original and *+1 Speed Keyboard Escape*. At least 15 "…for Eggs" or "Steal a…" clones made the top-100 peak list within about 8 weeks.
3. **This week's viral breakout is *DONT SPLASH*** (Oct 17). It is a one-rule horror obby ("don't touch the water"), with 48M visits and a 116K peak in 9 days, spread mainly by TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Halloween timing helped.
4. **Being viral and making money are not the same thing.** Roblox's Q2 bookings missed because "players moved away from 2025's high-spending viral games," and the CFO says current hits "don't monetize at last year's levels." The most consistent earners (RIVALS, Blox Fruits, Murder Mystery 2, Adopt Me!, Pet Sim 99, Tower Defense games) earn far more per player than the viral simulators.
5. **The rules changed in June 2026.** The Recommended For You algorithm now scores **28-day retention, first-60-second bounce, co-play and spend days** instead of 7-day spikes. A thumbnail-bait game with no depth now gets cut off after its first wave. To go viral now, a game needs a **hook that spreads plus the depth to keep players for 28 days.**

## 2. Platform context (what's changed in 2026)

| Factor | Detail | Why it matters to us |
|---|---|---|
| **DAU and bookings down** | DAU fell from 152M (Q3'25) to 132M (Q1'26) to 123M (Q2'26). Q3'26 bookings are guided **down 14–18% YoY**. Roblox stock is down about 35% YTD. | The audience is still huge but smaller. Roblox wants games that **retain and monetize**, and will reward them. |
| **Discovery algorithm rewrite (Jun 15, 2026)** | Recommended For You now uses a **28-day window** with day 1, day 2–7 and day 8–28 buckets. "Most Important" signals: play-through rate, **first-play bounce (<60s and 61–180s, negative)**, play days, playtime. "Important" signals: **intentional co-play days**, qualified sessions, **spend days**, Robux spent. Only organic Recommended For You joins count. | The **first minute** decides whether we get distributed. Daily return reasons, playing with friends, and a cheap first purchase are now algorithm inputs. |
| **Concentration is falling** | Top 10 games hold about 20% of hours, down from 30% three years ago. About 52% of CCU is in the top 50 games. | The mid-tier has more room than before. A new game can earn a lasting slot. |
| **Mandatory age checks** | 57% of global DAU age-checked. Chat restrictions for unverified users. Adults are nearly 1/3 of age-checked US DAU. | Social features that rely on text chat are weaker. Emotes, pings and co-op actions work better. |
| **Adults are the growth segment** | US users aged 18–34 grew 40–50% YoY and **monetize about 50% more** than under-18s. Since Jun 8, the **DevEx rate is +42%** for age-checked US 18+ spend in "novel" R15 games. A *Standout Games* home-page shelf was added. | There is a second, separate route to money: a deeper game aimed at teens and adults. |
| **Loot box rules** | Paid random items need **numeric odds shown before purchase**, must be gated with `PolicyService.ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` (also for trading), and are restricted in Brazil for under-18s and unverified users. Global since about May 2026 (driven by South Korea's law). | Paid egg or crate hatching must be built compliant from day one. Earning eggs with in-game currency is safer. |
| **Mechanic bans happen fast** | Steal an Egg launched with a "doomscrolling for rewards" feature (curated TikToks). Roblox took the game down within hours or a day and then banned the mechanic. | Avoid gray-area engagement hacks. A takedown in launch week kills momentum. |
| **IP risk** | Steal a Brainrot had to remove *Tung Tung Tung Sahur* during a licensing dispute. The many "Kukirin" scooter games use a real brand's name. | Use original characters. Memes are fine as a vibe, not as a trademark. |
| **New creation tools** | Roblox Build (mobile AI creation, July), Google Playground text-to-game (Oct 7), Roblox Everywhere export to Steam, consoles and web. | Cloning will get even faster. Our protection is **speed of execution and live-ops**, not the idea itself. |

## 5. Common factors across the winners

### A. Game-design factors

1. **The name is the whole pitch: "Verb a Noun" or "+1 X".**
   Steal an Egg, Ride a Pet, Kick a Lucky Block, Fake an Egg, Chop a Forest, Climb for Animals, Paint to Get Rich, Search for the Needle, +1 Speed Keyboard Escape, +1 Stone Skipping. A child can understand the game from the tile alone, in any language. This lowers the first-click barrier and sets correct expectations, which keeps first-play bounce low (an algorithm signal).

2. **Remix a proven loop rather than invent a new one ("evolution beats revolution").**
   Steal an Egg = Steal a Brainrot's steal-from-bases loop + Adopt Me/Pet Sim egg hatching + the "+1 speed" treadmill. Nearly every 2026 hit combines two or three loops players already know with one new noun or twist.

3. **Collect → passive income → upgrade → collect better.**
   Brainrots, pets, eggs, dinos, cats. There are rarity tiers, income keeps accruing between sessions, and stock rotates on a timer. This creates daily reasons to return, which feed the play-days and day 2–7 / day 8–28 retention signals.

4. **Low-stakes PvP friction ("someone can take your stuff").**
   Stealing creates emotion. Rage, revenge and the "kid cries after losing pet" clips are what made Steal a Brainrot spread on TikTok. Shields and timers keep it from being too punishing.

5. **One-rule premise that reads in a 3-second clip.**
   DONT SPLASH ("don't touch the water"), anomaly games ("spot what's wrong"), Huss Valley (runners vs catchers). The rule is the hook and the video thumbnail at the same time.

6. **Physics chaos and ragdolls.**
   Ragdoll Reality, Realistic Ragdoll Engine, Backseat Rally, Bike Universe, Flying Tesla Roadster, Build and Kill Zombies. They produce funny clips without anyone authoring the content.

7. **Instant progress in the first 60 seconds.**
   The "+1 every second" genre is the extreme version: numbers go up immediately. Roblox now penalizes bounces under 60 seconds and at 61–180 seconds, so this matters for distribution, not just for feel.

8. **Shipped simple, then updated live.**
   Grow a Garden's first version took about 3 days. Many breakouts have "[Testing]", "[BETA]" or "[ALPHA]" in the title. Steal an Egg reportedly ships weekly updates. **Speed to market beats polish** during a trend window.

### B. Live-ops factors

9. **Scheduled live events create synchronized peaks.**
   Weekly "Admin Abuse" events (Steal an Egg: Saturdays before the weekly update), the Grow a Garden × Steal a Brainrot "admin war" (helped set the 47.4M platform record), and Grow a Garden's update-day peaks (5M → 11.7M → 16M → 21.3M). Peaks drop fast afterward (21.3M → 7.6M within an hour), but each one recruits new players and gives creators something to film.

10. **Seasonal skins on everything.**
    About 60% of today's trending list has 🎃 tags. Title emoji tags like `[🎃]`, `[UPD]` and `[NEW STAGES]` act as free re-engagement and SEO.

### C. External factors

11. **Short-form video is the main way players find games.**
    50% of 10–15-year-old Roblox players discover games through YouTube, more than any other channel. TikTok "what's this game called" searches are a measurable demand signal (DONT SPLASH). Designing for a 3-second clip means the core moment has to look good in a vertical phone frame.

12. **Meme and culture timing.**
    Italian Brainrot (Steal a Brainrot), the brainrot tag still in titles (Brainrot SKYFALL, Brainrot Memory Bridge), "67" in titles, KPop Demon Hunters awareness (62% of Roblox players vs 40% of others). Riding an active meme gives a game free awareness.

13. **Fast-follow window of about 4–8 weeks.**
    Within weeks, egg spin-offs filled the top-100 list, and *Break and Steal an Egg* reached 1.3M. Newzoo counted 26 Roblox clones of MECCHA CHAMELEON within 3 weeks of its Steam launch. Once a trend has 15+ clones, the window has closed.

14. **Regional and cultural niches are underpriced.**
    Karinderya (Filipino, 157K), Arabic-language hits (صيد الضبّان 122K, Sarab 100K, شمهروش 2.1M visits), Kukirin (Poland/Europe scooter culture, with a "Kukirin Life Poland" spin-off), Thai Quiz Duels. Local games face less competition and build a loyal community.

15. **Seasonality of the Roblox calendar.**
    Horror peaks in October, and summer and winter school breaks are platform-wide peaks. The next big window is **US Thanksgiving through the New Year holidays**.

## 6. Competitive read and where the openings are

| Lane | Status | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Steal/egg/pet idle tycoons | **Saturated.** 15+ clones; the original at 726K and falling from 14M. | Too late to clone directly. The *ingredients* (collect, steal, passive income) are still proven, so use them with a different, fresh noun. |
| "+1 per second" incrementals | Saturated but still producing hits weekly (+1 Venom, +1 Pickaxe…) | Cheap to make and fast to test, but monetization and retention are shallow. Better as a hook layer than as the whole game. |
| One-rule horror obbies and anomaly games | **Hot right now**, Halloween-boosted | Window closes after Oct 31. Expect a slump in November, then horror returns for summer and next October. |
| Physics chaos co-op (driving, ragdoll) | Rising (Backseat Rally, Bike Universe, Ragdoll Reality) | Aligns with the co-play signal and clips well. Few have deep progression yet, so **there's room for one that does.** |
| Collectible battlers and tower defense | Mid-size CCU, **top-tier spend per player** (Cameraman TD, Vanguards, Cat Battles) | Best monetization per player. Players expect a lot of content. |
| Competitive shooters | Dominated by RIVALS | High skill bar, hard to unseat. |
| Roleplay, fashion, makeup | Steady (DTI, Facecard spinning off a Junior version) | Big, mostly female audience. Monetizes through cosmetics. |
| 18+ "novel" games | Thin supply. Roblox is paying +42% DevEx and has a Standout Games shelf. | Strategic tailwind, but higher production bar and slower growth. |
| Localized cultural sims | Underserved | Low competition, loyal audiences, cheap to theme. |

Vendor reports (ROLearn) list simulators, obbies and tycoons as "highly saturated, avoid." **The real charts contradict this:** most 2026 breakouts *are* simulators and obbies. Saturation is not what kills a game. What kills it is arriving late to a trend without a fresh noun or twist.

## 7. Design principles for our game

These follow directly from sections 2–6:

1. **Hook:** a "Verb a Noun" title plus a premise that reads in a 3-second clip, either a one-rule mechanic or a funny or dramatic social moment.
2. **Core:** combine at least two proven loops (for example collect + steal, run + collect, co-op + physics). Make one thing fresh: the noun, the setting, or a twist.
3. **First 60 seconds:** no menus or tutorial walls. Visible progress in the first 10 seconds, and the core moment within 60.
4. **Retention spine:** passive or offline income, timed stock rotations, daily rewards, rarity collection, and a long-term goal (rebirth or prestige) to cover the day 8–28 window.
5. **Social:** design for playing with friends (party bonuses, co-op objectives) and for low-stakes rivalry. Don't depend on text chat.
6. **Monetization:** early cheap purchase (starter pack / 2× gamepass) to hit "spend days"; tiered gamepasses; cosmetics; any paid randomness must be odds-disclosed and `PolicyService`-gated. Aim for p75–p90 ARPPU ($1.25–$2.33).
7. **Live-ops from week one:** weekly update and a scheduled event (Admin Abuse-style). Plan seasonal content for the holidays.
8. **Ship fast:** an MVP in weeks, not months, then iterate on live data (bounce, D1, play-through in Creator Analytics).
9. **Distribution plan:** short-form clip pipeline (TikTok/Shorts) from launch day, a Discord, Roblox ads budget for the first player cohort, and outreach to mid-size Roblox YouTubers.
10. **Avoid:** others' IP and brand names, engagement hacks Roblox might ban, and pay-to-win steal mechanics that drive backlash.

### Starting directions for brainstorming (not decisions)

- **A. Fresh-noun steal/collect tycoon.** The Steal a Brainrot / Steal an Egg engine with a new collectible and a twist (for example, collectibles that evolve when stolen). Proven, but the lane is crowded. Win only with a better noun and faster live-ops.
- **B. Co-op physics chaos with progression.** Backseat Rally / Bike Universe energy, plus collect-and-upgrade depth and party bonuses. Clips well, fits the co-play signal, and the lane is less crowded.
- **C. One-rule social horror or anomaly for the winter season.** A DONT SPLASH-style rule game with a holiday theme, plus collectibles for retention. Timed for the Dec–Jan break.
- **D. Collectible battler / TD with a viral front door.** A "Verb a Noun" hook leading into a Cat Battles / Cameraman TD style economy. Highest spend per player, highest content load.
- **E. Localized cultural sim.** A Karinderya-style game for an underserved language or culture. Low competition, but a lower ceiling.
